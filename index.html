Clear boundaries.

Real scoring logic.

A deterministic engine combines recent form, playing patterns and earlier referee assignments, including experience with comparable league stages and table stakes.

02

Ask and compare.

Ask why one referee leads, compare their score factors side by side, or ask RefMatch to exclude a candidate and show the new result.

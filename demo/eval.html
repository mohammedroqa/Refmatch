      05 / Review independently
      
            
      A review trail, not a stamp.
      
          
      
          
      
            
          
      
        
      
        
      
          Use the blind pack before opening reference answers. Domain and
          technical reviews need different people. A disagreement requires a
          third reviewer. Local declarations do not verify anyone’s identity or
          expertise.

      Method and limits
      
        
      Make the result reproducible.
      
        
      
          Unsafe clearance includes both invalid plans and plans with unresolved
          checks labelled valid. Error and unrun counts remain visible.
          End-to-end accuracy uses every planned case as its denominator.
          Repeated trials are reported separately.
        
      
        
      
          Intervals resample entire case families 500 times. They are
          descriptive 95% bootstrap intervals, not proof of real-world
          performance. Related synthetic families may still share patterns.
          Small-sample probability scores do not establish calibration.
        
      
        
      
          Evidence reading measures extracted statuses, supporting citations,
          and the deterministic verdict obtained from those facts. The
          rule-audit task supplies reference facts to isolate rule application.
          Expert review remains a separate process.
        
      
        
      
          For controlled challenges: generate a private corpus after freezing
          your prompt, keep it out of version control, run the local CLI, then
          archive settings and the report. Never describe public validation as
          unseen.

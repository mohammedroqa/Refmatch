    Three years of evidence. Every assumption visible.

    A rolling 36-month window ends at 00:00 UTC on the planning date. Recent team patterns contribute 60%; historical patterns contribute 40%, with a 365-day half-life. The scorer uses fouls (25%), recorded cards (20%), shots (15%), comparable table situations (20%), evidence volume (10%) and team-assignment spacing (10%). Five complete earlier matches per team and referee are required.

    Standings use the current season only. Promoted clubs can have limited Premier League history. Passes, duels and decision accuracy are not available in this source. Similar appointments measure exposure, not successful decisions. The minimum gap, spacing preferences and weights are app assumptions.

    Source records are captured when refreshed; they do not archive the exact time a historical correction was published. Result dates control the evidence cutoff. The exported brief includes the source checksums, evidence, model rules, committee inputs and draft.

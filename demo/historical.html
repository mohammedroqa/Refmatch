    Data and method

    Fit combines the teams’ recent and season-long playing patterns with referee appointment profiles, experience in similar league stages and table situations, and team-assignment spacing. Weights, minimum samples, and the 28-day team-spacing preference are app assumptions, not competition policy. A larger sample is not a measure of referee ability. Other competitions, current availability, private conflicts, and official eligibility are absent.

    Source quality notes

    Historical sources: Pappalardo and Massucco’s Events and Matches datasets, described in Pappalardo et al. (2019), licensed CC BY 4.0. Real names come from the attributed Teams and Referees files. Card tags use the published tag mapping. Profiles use only records before 00:00 UTC on the planning date. Target match outcomes and actual appointments are withheld. Card-tagged events and recorded event volumes are source-specific proxies.

    Law context: Law 5 — The Referee ↗ describes referee duties and advantage; Law 12 — Fouls and Misconduct ↗ covers offences and sanctions. Neither sets referee assignment scores.
